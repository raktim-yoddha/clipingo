# Changelog

Every version, newest first. This project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org); before 1.0, a breaking
change bumps the minor number.

## Unreleased — Vertical clips that fit any video, and a real editor
- A timeline editor with an in-app player: split, trim, reorder and stitch parts, with undo
- Vertical clips choose a layout per shot: follow a person, two people stacked, face and game stacked (swap them either way), everyone in a band, or the whole frame
- Smart zoom pushes in on the strong lines
- Captions keep off faces, sit on the seam of a stack and match the voice much more closely
- Cancelling a job now really stops it
- Library shows thumbnails and can remove a video
- Face tracking is saved with the video, so opening a clip never scans again
- The window is now opaque gray with acrylic panels, and pages start right beside the sidebar
- The installer drops from 27.6 MB to about 10 MB and the app starts about twice as fast
- Leaving the editor frees its video players instead of keeping them in memory
